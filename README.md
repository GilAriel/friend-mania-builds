# Friend Mania

An isometric co-op roguelite for 1 to 4 friends, run by a snarky AI Dungeon Master.

## Play in your browser

**[Play Friend Mania in the browser](https://gilariel.github.io/friend-mania-builds/)** (Chrome or Edge work best). Nothing to install. It's couch co-op on one screen: plug in up to 2 controllers, or use the keyboard. Click the game once so it can play sound. Online co-op needs the downloaded game below.

## Get the game

Go to **[the latest release](../../releases/latest)** and download the zip for your computer, under **Assets**:

- **Windows:** `FriendMania-Windows.zip`. Unzip it into a new, empty folder and run `FriendMania.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** `FriendMania-Mac.zip`. Unzip it, right-click `Friend Mania.app`, choose **Open**, then **Open** again (first time only).
- **Linux:** `FriendMania-Linux.zip`. Unzip it and run `FriendMania.x86_64`.

Always unzip a new version into a new folder, never over an old one.

## Connect a controller

PlayStation (DualSense, DualShock 4) and Xbox pads both work. You can connect one before or after starting the game.

- **With a cable:** plug it into a USB port. That's it.
- **Bluetooth, PlayStation 5 pad:** hold **PS** + **Create** (the small button left of the touchpad) until the light flashes fast. **PlayStation 4 pad:** hold **PS** + **Share**.
- **Bluetooth, Xbox pad:** turn it on, then hold the small pair button on top until the Xbox logo flashes fast.
- Then on **Windows:** Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device > Bluetooth, and pick "Wireless Controller" (or "Xbox Wireless Controller"). On a **Mac:** System Settings > Bluetooth, and click Connect next to it.

The first pad is Player 1, a second pad is Player 2. The on-screen buttons switch to match your pad.

**No sound after connecting a PlayStation pad?** Windows sometimes makes the pad its speaker. The game switches back to your speakers by itself, or pick them in the menu (Options) > Settings > Sound device.

## Play together online

Everyone needs the same version.

1. Everyone installs **Tailscale** (free): https://tailscale.com/download. The host invites friends to their Tailscale network.
2. **Host:** on the War Table, pick **Host** (top-left). Your address shows there. Give friends the one that starts with `100.`.
3. **Friends:** pick **Join**, type the host's address on the number pad, then **JOIN**. Next time, **Rejoin** remembers it.

On the same Wi-Fi, the `192.168...` address works without Tailscale.

## Controls

A PlayStation or Xbox pad is the best way to play. Esc or Options opens the menu, and its **Controls** tab lists every button.
